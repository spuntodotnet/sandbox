## Memory

Every VMM runs in `vms.slice`, whose `MemoryMax` is what all boxes may take together (the installer
sets it to the machine's RAM − 2 GB). A box's size is a ceiling (its unit's `MemoryMax` is its RAM +
256 MB for the VMM), not a reservation: a box only holds what its guest touches, and what the guest
frees goes back (a balloon that never inflates, for its *free page reporting*).

So a machine is **overcommitted** on purpose — and protected by an **admission** before every
create, start and build:

1. **Real room**: the slice's free memory (its droppable page cache counted as free, bounded by the
   machine's `MemAvailable`) must be at least an eighth of the box's RAM, 1 GB at least.
2. **Announced room**: the sizes of the running boxes, plus this one, must stay under the slice's
   ceiling times `SPUNTO_FC_OVERCOMMIT` (1.5). A guest can grow into all of its size later; past this
   ratio the kernel would have to kill one.

Refused, the call fails with `capacity` (HTTP 409) and says why — never a box started that the OOM
killer takes a minute later. What was admitted is held for 30 s, so a burst of creates cannot all
take the same free memory. A box the OOM killer took anyway is `error`, with the reason.

`SPUNTO_FC_ADMIT=0` turns the admission off; `SPUNTO_FC_ADMIT_MIN_MB`, `SPUNTO_FC_OVERCOMMIT` tune it.

## CPU

On every VMM's unit:

- `CPUQuota` = its vCPUs + 0.5 (the VMM's own threads): never more than its size, even on an idle machine;
- `CPUWeight=100` for every box, whatever its size: under contention, each box that wants CPU gets
  an equal share, up to its vCPUs. A weight per vCPU would have given an 8-vCPU box eight times its
  1-vCPU neighbour's share — what happened before, measured.

`SPUNTO_FC_CPU_LIMITS=0`, `SPUNTO_FC_CPU_MARGIN`, `SPUNTO_FC_CPU_WEIGHT` (`vcpu` for a weight per vCPU).

## Network

Each box's traffic goes through two IFB devices (`fcifbu` up, `fcifbd` down) with one HTB class per
box: a ceiling per box and per direction (`SPUNTO_FC_NET_MBIT`, 200 Mbit/s), and when the machine's
part is full (`SPUNTO_FC_NET_TOTAL_MBIT`, 95 % of the link's speed), **equal shares per box** in
bytes, however many flows each opens. The uplink's own queue is never touched. `SPUNTO_FC_NET_LIMITS=0`
turns it off.

## Disks

A box's two disks are sparse ZFS volumes: they take what is written, lz4-compressed. Their size is
their bound — a guest cannot write past its disk's end. `grow` stops at `SPUNTO_FC_MAX_ROOT_GB` (200)
and `SPUNTO_FC_MAX_WS_GB` (500). Below `SPUNTO_FC_MIN_POOL_FREE_GB` (5) left in the pool, no new box
and no new memory file: a full pool would fail every box's writes.

## What a box uses

`sbx usage <box>` (`GET /v1/sandboxes/{id}/usage`) — measured on the host, not by the guest: CPU time
of its unit, its memory (with and without the droppable cache), its veth's bytes, its two volumes' IO
from ZFS's own counters. Cumulative counters: take differences.
